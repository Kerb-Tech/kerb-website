Estate Agent Technology

Turn every board into a lead.

Kerb attaches a dynamic QR code to your for-sale boards. Buyers scan, you capture — a warm lead from someone standing outside the door. Your board, your data, your leads.

Why Kerb

The board outside your listing is your most underused asset.

Thousands of people walk past your boards every week. Without Kerb, they forget who the agent is, forget the address, and do nothing more. With Kerb they scan the code and land on a Kerb-hosted property page with a photo of the property, your contact information and a direct link to your hosted property page where they can either click through to view the full listing and submit an enquiry or submit an enquiry directly via Kerb — two ways to capture the lead.

Capture the kerb browser

The buyer standing outside the property is your hottest lead. Kerb captures their details and forwards them straight to you.

Real scan analytics

See how many people stopped outside each property, when, and what they did next. Data you can show vendors at valuation.

You own the leads

Every name, number and email goes straight to you. No portal takes a cut. No algorithm decides who sees your data.
