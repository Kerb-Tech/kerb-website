1. About Kerb

Kerb Technology provides a board management platform for estate agents. The platform enables agents to generate dynamic QR-coded boards, link each board to one of their property listings, present scanning buyers with a Kerb-hosted property page (showing photos, listing details, and the agent's contact information), capture buyer enquiries through that page on behalf of the agent, redirect through to the agent's own listing where the buyer chooses, and track scan and enquiry analytics.

5. Scan Analytics, Property Pages & Buyer Enquiries

  • When a buyer scans a QR code, Kerb records anonymised analytics (timestamp, country/city level geo derived at scan-time without storing the IP, device type from the browser user agent) and presents them with a Kerb-hosted property page that displays the listing details, photos, and your contact information. Kerb does not use device fingerprinting or any persistent identifier that would track an individual across visits.
  • From that page the buyer can either click through to your own listing URL or submit an enquiry to you through Kerb.
  • Where a buyer submits the enquiry form, Kerb captures their name, email address, optional phone number and optional free-text message, and stores the enquiry against your account so you can review and respond. Full details of what is collected and the lawful basis are set out in our Privacy Policy.
  • You become joint controller for any enquiry submitted via your boards and are responsible for following up under your own privacy notice and for compliance with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. The essence of the joint controller arrangement (Article 26 UK GDPR) is: Kerb is responsible for capturing the enquiry on the property page and transmitting it to you; you are responsible for all substantive follow-up communication with the buyer and any downstream processing.
  • Buyers who do not submit the enquiry form remain anonymous to Kerb beyond the analytics data above.
