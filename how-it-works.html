How It Works

Up and running in a few days.

Everything is managed from one simple dashboard. No technical knowledge required.

Step
1

Set Up

  • → Create your branch account online in minutes
  • → Generate and name your boards in Board Management
  • → No software to install
Step
2

Add Your Listings

  • → Upload your properties via Excel — paste a hyperlinked or URL address and Kerb pulls in the information
  • → Price, postcode & town are auto-detect from the listing URL — only the Property Ref is required
  • → Allocate a QR code to each property in one click from the properties page or the board management page
  • → Preview your listing to see exactly what the buyer sees
Step
3

Go Live

  • → Print and attach the Kerb QR code to your for-sale board (see options below)
  • → From that moment, every scan is captured and tracked
  • → Buyers scan and have instant access to the property information — no app, no login
  • → They can either click through to your full listing directly or submit an enquiry to you via Kerb — either way, you receive a lead alert
Always
∞

Manage & Win

  • → Property sold — Delete the property (auto resets the stats for that QR code) and reassign the QR code to the next property
  • → View scan counts, peak times and repeat visitors in your dashboard any time
  • → Use real scan data in your vendor valuations
Getting the QR on Your Board

3 ways to do it.

Pick the option that works for your current board stock — or mix and match.

Option A · Best Quality

Printed directly onto the board

Included in the board artwork file sent to your manufacturer. UV-resistant, weatherproof, and perfectly integrated.

Option B · Most Flexible

Weatherproof vinyl sticker

Outdoor-grade vinyl with UV laminate. Retrofits your existing boards — no reprinting required. From just 50p per board.

Option C · Early Adoption

Laminated QR card

A laminated card you can cable-tie or clip to any board. Perfect for getting started quickly — no permanent modification needed.
