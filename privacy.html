Buyers / Scanners

When a board is scanned, the buyer lands on a Kerb-hosted property page that displays the listing details, photos, and the agent's contact information. From that page the buyer can either click through to the agent's own listing or submit an enquiry directly to the agent through Kerb. Where a buyer chooses to submit an enquiry, we collect:

DataPurposeLawful Basis
NameForwarding the enquiry to the agentConsent (the buyer voluntarily submits the form)
Email addressForwarding the enquiry to the agent and confirming receiptConsent
Phone number (optional)Forwarding the enquiry to the agent if the buyer prefers a call backConsent
Free-text message (optional)Forwarding the enquiry to the agentConsent
Property reference scannedLinking the enquiry to the correct listingConsent

Enquiries are stored against the agent's account so the agent can review and respond. The agent who owns the board becomes the joint controller for any enquiry submitted via that board and is responsible for following up under their own privacy notice. Under Article 26 UK GDPR, the essence of the arrangement is: Kerb is responsible for capturing the enquiry on the property page and transmitting it to the agent; the agent is responsible for all substantive follow-up communication with the buyer and any downstream processing under their own privacy notice. Buyers who do not submit the enquiry form remain anonymous to Kerb beyond the analytics data described below.

Payment Data

When you subscribe to a paid plan, payment is processed securely by Stripe. We do not store your full card number, CVV, or bank details — these are handled entirely by Stripe in their PCI DSS Level 1 certified environment. We receive only a confirmation of payment status, plan type, and a Stripe customer ID to link your subscription to your account.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the Kerb platform
  • To authenticate estate agent accounts
  • To display the agent's listing on a Kerb-hosted property page when a board is scanned, and to redirect through to the agent's own listing where the buyer chooses
  • To capture buyer enquiries submitted on the property page and forward them to the relevant estate agent
  • To provide scan and enquiry analytics and reporting to estate agents
  • To communicate service updates and support information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

We will only send you service-related communications (account, billing, support, and platform updates) on the basis of our contract with you. We will not send you marketing emails or other electronic marketing communications unless you have given us explicit opt-in consent, in line with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR). You can withdraw that consent at any time by emailing connect@kerb.technology or using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.

We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Article 22 UK GDPR.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Application data is stored on encrypted servers hosted by Vercel (US) and Neon (US). Property-listing scraping infrastructure runs on Hetzner (EU — Germany / Finland).
  • International transfers are covered by the UK-US Data Bridge agreement
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt (industry standard, irreversible)
  • All connections use TLS/SSL encryption
  • Property photos are stored in Vercel Blob Storage with access controls

5. Data Retention

  • Agent accounts: Retained while the account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account closure request.
  • Buyer enquiries: Retained against the agent's account for up to 90 days from the date of submission so the agent can review and respond. Older enquiries are automatically deleted. Buyers may request earlier deletion at any time by emailing connect@kerb.technology.
  • Analytics data: Retained for up to 24 months in anonymised/aggregated form.

6. Your Rights

Under the UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interest

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at connect@kerb.technology. We will respond within one calendar month, as required by UK GDPR. Where a request is particularly complex or where you have made several requests, we may extend this by up to a further two months and will let you know within the first month.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Kerb uses only strictly necessary cookies for authentication and session management on the agent dashboard. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies. We also do not use device fingerprinting, local storage trackers, or any other similar technology that would require consent under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).
